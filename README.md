
# Social Media Engagement Analytics


# Project Objective :
To analyze social media engagement data by importing, cleaning, and transforming the dataset, then applying statistical and visualization techniques (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly) to uncover insights into audience demographics, content performance, sentiment impact, and engagement trends. The goal is to identify patterns that drive higher reach and interaction, optimize content strategies, and provide actionable recommendations for improving social media performance.

# Data Sources :
Data Source from Github: https://github.com/GeethaGunasekaran1/Dataset_rep/blob/main/social_media_engagement_5000.csv








# Summary :


Engagement Metrics
Post Type: Video posts tend to attract higher average likes compared to text/image posts.
Country Reach: Impressions are concentrated in a few key countries, highlighting where your audience is strongest.
Sentiment: Positive posts correlate with higher follower counts and engagement rates.

# Audience Demographics
Gender: Engagement is skewed toward one gender group (pie chart shows imbalance).
Age: Histogram reveals clustering in younger age brackets, suggesting campaigns resonate more with youth audiences.
Trends & Behavior
Daily Engagement: Line charts show spikes around specific dates, likely tied to campaigns or events.
Device Usage: Swarm plots indicate mobile users drive more engagement than desktop users.
Content Features
Hashtags: Posts with more hashtags generally show higher impressions, but overuse may dilute impact.
Followers vs Sentiment: Violin plots show positive sentiment posts attract broader follower distribution.

# Statistical Highlights
Central Tendency: Mean likes and shares are skewed by a few viral posts; median values better represent typical performance.
Spread: High variance in engagement metrics shows uneven distribution of audience attention.
Correlations: Strong positive correlation between impressions and likes; weaker correlation between comments and watch_time.


# Conclusion:
The analysis of your social media engagement dataset highlights several key takeaways:
Content Strategy: Visual posts, especially videos, consistently outperform text-based content in terms of likes and shares.
Audience Demographics: Engagement is concentrated among younger age groups and shows a skewed gender distribution, suggesting targeted content strategies could improve reach.
Geographic Insights: Impressions are clustered in specific countries, making geo-targeted campaigns a strong opportunity for growth.
Behavioral Trends: Mobile users drive higher engagement, and daily activity patterns reveal spikes around campaigns or events.
Sentiment & Hashtags: Positive sentiment posts correlate with follower growth, while hashtags amplify reach but should be used strategically to avoid dilution.
Statistical Findings: Engagement metrics are highly skewed — a few viral posts drive averages, so median values are more reliable indicators. Strong correlations exist between impressions, likes, and follower counts, confirming that reach is the primary driver of engagement.
