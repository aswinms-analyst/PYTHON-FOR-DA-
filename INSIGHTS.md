# 📈 Key Insights
Content Performance

🎥 Video posts consistently generate the highest average likes and shares.

🖼️ Image posts perform moderately, while text posts lag in engagement.

# Audience Demographics

👥 Engagement is skewed toward younger age groups, with a noticeable gender imbalance.

🌍 Impressions are concentrated in a few countries, suggesting strong regional audience clusters.

# Behavioral Trends

📱 Mobile users drive significantly higher engagement compared to desktop users.

📅 Daily engagement trends show spikes around campaigns/events, revealing timing as a key factor.

# Content Features

#️⃣ Posts with hashtags achieve higher impressions, but excessive use reduces effectiveness.

😀 Positive sentiment posts correlate with follower growth and stronger engagement rates.

# Statistical Findings

A few viral posts skew averages, making median values more reliable indicators.

Strong correlations exist between impressions, likes, and followers, confirming reach as the main driver of engagement.
